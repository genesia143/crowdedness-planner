      How to read the estimated crowd comparison

      The crowd indicator is an experimental estimate from mobile e-tickets and scheduled buses. Low means fewer recorded mobile tickets per scheduled bus; high means more. It uses the same fixed ranges for every option. It assumes similar mobile-ticket use across routes and may use a route-wide average. The estimate can be wrong.

      This is a tentative comparison, not a count of people on board or a prediction of seat availability. An unavailable estimate does not mean the bus is empty.

      Journey times come from the timetable, not live bus positions. Map-based walking uses OpenStreetMap; simplified routing estimates straight-line distances. Some suitable options may be missed by the routing search.
