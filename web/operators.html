Where buses are under most pressure

For bus operators and planners: the route stretches and routes with the most recorded mobile-ticket passengers per scheduled bus, by day type and time of day. Use it to identify routes and hours for further investigation; the data cannot establish actual crowding or whether extra buses are needed.

Pressure is matched mobile-ticket passengers per scheduled bus in October 2026 training data. Only part of all riders use mobile tickets, and ranking different routes assumes they use them about equally. Treat this as a guide to where to look first, then confirm with counts on the ground.
